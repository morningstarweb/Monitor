1. PRINCIPLES & SCOPE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Morning Star Web is committed to full transparency, data
minimization, and privacy-by-design under:
- India Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act 2023)
- EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR - Article 5 & Article 6)
- UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR)

2. ZERO SURVEILLANCE & LOCAL PROCESSING GUARANTEE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Monitor operates under a strict zero-surveillance architecture.
- We do not collect, monetize, sell, or profile your personal data.
- System metrics (CPU, memory, storage partitions, running processes, and battery status)
  flow directly between your workstation and paired mobile devices.
- Git repository contents, source files, and commit DAG graphs are processed
  locally on device and are never uploaded to any cloud repository server.

3. DATA MINIMIZATION & GDPR ARTICLE 5 PRINCIPLES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
In accordance with GDPR Article 5 principles:
- Art. 5(1)(a) Lawful, fair, and transparent processing.
- Art. 5(1)(b) Purpose limitation strictly for developer workstation observability.
- Art. 5(1)(c) Data minimisation (strictly limited to hardware metrics and session hashes).
- Art. 5(1)(d) Accuracy of hardware telemetry.
- Art. 5(1)(e) Storage limitation (transient snapshots).
- Art. 5(1)(f) Integrity and confidentiality via AES-256 and TLS 1.3.
- Zero-Surveillance and Local-First guarantees: Zero code uploads.
- Temporary session tokens stored in hardware-backed keystores (Windows DPAPI / Direct AndroidKeyStore AES-256-GCM).
- Optional Google OAuth subject identifier if you choose to enable the remote
  Cloudflare edge broker for out-of-home synchronization.

4. USER RIGHTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Under the DPDP Act 2023 and GDPR, you retain complete rights to:
- Access and inspect any data processed by the application.
- Revoke pairings and delete session tokens instantly via the Settings menu.
- Complete data erasure upon unpairing or uninstalling the application.
